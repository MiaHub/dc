The sport I keep coming back to. Notes to myself, mostly about the things I know and still
fail to do.

### Why it works as exercise

It is the rare activity where the effort is a by-product. Nobody has to motivate themselves
to chase a ball — the chasing is the point, and the conditioning arrives without being the
goal. Compare that to a treadmill, where the effort is the entire experience.

It is also intermittent: short sprints, direction changes, recovery between points. That
pattern is much closer to what a body is built for than a steady hour of anything.

And it is social and scheduled. Someone else is waiting on the court, which beats willpower
every time.

### Footwork is the whole game

Almost every bad shot I hit is a footwork problem wearing a technique costume. The racket
was fine; I was in the wrong place and out of time.

- **Split step** as the opponent strikes. It feels like wasted motion and it is what lets
  you move in any direction
- **Recover** after every shot. The point is not over because you hit the ball
- **Small adjusting steps** at the end, not one big lunge — the last half-metre decides
  whether you are balanced
- Get there **early** enough to be waiting for the ball rather than reaching for it

### The warm-up I skip and regret

Tennis is explosive and one-sided, which is a bad combination for joints. Shoulders, elbows,
knees, lower back.

Ten minutes: easy movement to raise the temperature, some rotation through the hips and
upper back, shoulders through their range, then start the hitting gently instead of opening
with serves. Serving cold is how shoulders get hurt.

The asymmetry is the argument for [strength work]({{ site.baseurl }}/2025/04/06/Strength.html)
away from the court — one arm and one rotation direction doing all the work, twice a week,
for years, needs a counterweight.

### The mental part

The score is designed to make a single point recoverable and a lapse in attention expensive.
Which means the useful skill is not hitting winners, it is how quickly you let go of the last
mistake.

That transfers. The habit of losing a point, taking a breath and playing the next one is the
same habit as a failed deployment at 17:00.

### Note to self

Play the percentage shot. The spectacular one works perhaps one time in five, and I remember
that one and forget the four.
